01Position // where I stand
▲ the default

The record of what you do is the most valuable thing you own, and it is taken by default. Collected without asking, correlated across sources you never linked yourself, retained indefinitely, and secured as an afterthought.

It is not a conspiracy. It is just the cheapest architecture, and nobody was ever asked to approve it.

◆ my answer

I work on the afterthought — and I intend to make the first two parts harder. Every dragnet is software, and software has authors, dependencies, memory bugs and a threat model somebody wrote at 3am.

So I build scanners that catch flaws before release, study the cryptography that keeps data useless to whoever takes it, and treat privacy as a property you engineer, not a checkbox you ship.
